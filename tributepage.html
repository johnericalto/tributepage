Born in 1989 · Cebu, Philippines

About My Mother

Elizabeth is a sales associate who has dedicated her life to marketing. But to me, she is so much more. She is my first teacher, my safest place, and the person who knows me better than anyone.

She is more than just my mother. She is my comfort, my guide, and one of the biggest reasons I keep going. No matter how much I grow, there will always be a part of me that finds home in her.

Her Beautiful Qualities

Three words describe the woman I look up to most:

  • Responsible — She faces her responsibilities and does what needs to be done.
  • Understanding — She tries to understand me, even when I struggle to explain myself.
  • Hardworking — She keeps going and gives her best for the people she loves.

Things She Loves

  • Keeping the house clean and in order.
  • Enjoying good food and the simple pleasures of life.
  • Lovingly nagging me, because that is one of the ways she shows that she cares.

Sometimes, the little things that make us laugh or roll our eyes become the memories we miss the most.

A Memory I'll Never Forget

She always checks up on me. Somehow, she sees through me. She knows what I feel even before I say a word. Sometimes, I do not even need to explain myself because she already senses that something is wrong.

It feels like she is the one person who truly knows me, even the parts of myself I cannot put into words. That kind of understanding is something I will always treasure.

Her Greatest Achievements

Some achievements cannot be measured by awards, money, or recognition. For me, one of my mother's greatest achievements is the way she stood up for me when the world felt like it was against me.

She was always there. She defended me, supported me, and reminded me that I was not alone. Her presence has been one of the greatest gifts of my life.

Her Biggest Challenge, My Greatest Pride

My mother gave birth to me at 17, at a time when nothing felt ready. She was still so young, with so much of her own life ahead of her. She could have chosen a different path, one that favored her own comfort, but she chose accountability and raised me with love.

I know that growing up and becoming a mother so young could not have been easy. There were probably sacrifices I did not understand and struggles I could not see. Yet she continued to show up for me.

That is why I am deeply proud of her. I admire the woman she has become, the strength she has shown, and the love she has given me. When I think about the person I want to become, I think of her.

She is proof that even when life does not go as planned, love, courage, and responsibility can make a beautiful difference.

To My First Home

You once told me that life isn't about what you have, but what you do with it. You lived that truth — at 17, with nothing ready, you chose me.

Thank you for every sacrifice, every value, every bit of love. I see it all, and I will treasure it forever. Thank you for checking on me, understanding me even when I cannot explain myself, and standing beside me when I need someone the most.

I may not always say it, and I may not always show it perfectly, but I hope you know how much you mean to me. Everything you have taught me will stay with me as I grow and find my own way in life.

If I become even half the person you are, I will be proud of who I have become. You have given me more than I could ever put into words.

You mean the world to me. I love you, Ma. ♡
